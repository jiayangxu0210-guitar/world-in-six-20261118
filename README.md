**The World in Six — 18 November 2026** is a single illustrated front page for six developments reported that Wednesday. The image is drawn by `draw.py` (Pillow + DejaVu fonts, no network, no randomness), and the Node 22 entry point `main.ts` copies the finished poster to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

To redraw the poster from scratch:

```sh
python3 draw.py
```

## Reporting notes

- **Shenzhen:** Xi Jinping greeted Donald Trump at the airport on November 18 as Trump arrived for APEC. Three-way talks with Vladimir Putin were scheduled for November 19; the image does not present them as completed. [DW](https://www.dw.com/en/apec-summit-xi-trump-and-putin-gather-in-shenzhen-china/live-79964719)
- **Interstellar space:** NASA's Voyager 1 reached a distance of one light-day at 02:16 PST on November 18. That is about 25.9 billion kilometers, or 24 hours for a one-way radio signal. [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/where-are-voyager-1-and-voyager-2-now/); [CNN's report after the milestone](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/18/science/voyager-1-light-day)
- **Strait of Hormuz:** On November 18, ADNOC Logistics & Services reported that one of its vessels had been attacked on the evening of November 17. One crew member was missing and 11 were injured as of that report. [Emirates News Agency via Aletihad](https://en.aletihad.ae/news/business/4706755/adnoc-l-s-says-vessel-attacked-while-transiting-strait-of-ho)
- **European Union:** The Council and European Parliament announced a provisional deal to speed permits for renewable energy and power grids. The image does not imply the deal is already law. [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/18/grids-package-council-and-parliament-agree-to-streamline-permitting-for-a-faster-clean-energy-transition/)
- **Horn of Africa:** Reuters reported on November 18, citing sources, that Ethiopia and Eritrea were massing forces along their border. The image shows the reported buildup, not an outbreak of war. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/ethiopia-eritrea-amass-forces-along-border-heightening-war-fears-sources-say-2026-11-18/)
- **Peru:** IGP recorded an offshore magnitude 6.0 earthquake at 01:07 local time on November 18, 96 km west of San Juan de Marcona in the Ica region. [IGP bulletin as reproduced by RPP](https://rpp.pe/peru/ica/ica-sismo-de-magnitud-60-remecio-la-region-esta-madrugada-noticia-1718321)
